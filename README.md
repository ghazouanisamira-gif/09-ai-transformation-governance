# AI Transformation & Governance

**Anonymized Client Case Study | 2024–2026**

A strategic transformation case study demonstrating how AI can be structured, governed and scaled within complex customer operations.

This evidence focuses on **AI transformation, operational governance, knowledge management, quality assurance, customer experience analytics, human oversight and change management** — not on AI software development.

## What this case demonstrates

- AI readiness and operational landscape assessment
- Multi-provider governance and standardisation
- Knowledge-base transformation for AI use
- Risk-based deployment sequencing
- Response/macro structuring and version control
- Integration of AI into quality management
- AI-assisted CSAT root-cause analysis
- Automated deviation detection within analytical processes
- Human oversight, escalation and validation
- Change management and adoption

## Challenge

The environment involved multiple customer-service channels, operational providers and existing knowledge resources. AI adoption therefore required more than deploying a technology solution: it required a common operating model, reliable knowledge, validation criteria, governance mechanisms and controlled deployment.

## Objectives

1. Establish a common framework for AI-related operational decisions.
2. Improve the reliability and usability of the knowledge base.
3. Introduce AI progressively according to risk and operational readiness.
4. Standardise validation and escalation across participating providers.
5. Extend AI capabilities beyond automation into quality, CSAT and analytics.
6. Maintain clear human accountability throughout the transformation.
